# Privacy Policy

**JASDA - Just A Simple Dice App**

JASDA does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data. At all.

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics or telemetry.
- No advertising or tracking of any kind.
- No cookies.
- No network access — the app never connects to the internet.
- Nothing is written to disk beyond what iOS itself manages for any app.

Every roll happens on your device and stays there. The app's App Store
privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"**, and it ships with an Apple
privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) declaring the same.

This policy can only ever get shorter.
